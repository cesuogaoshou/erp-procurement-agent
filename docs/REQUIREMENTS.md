# Requirements

## Product Goal

Build an ERP intelligent procurement decision system that analyzes future material shortages, recommends suppliers, creates purchase drafts, waits for human approval, and records evidence.

## Must Have

- Minimal ERP data model and reproducible simulated data.
- Inventory query, demand baseline forecast, shortage calculation, supplier four-metric scoring, and Top-3 recommendation.
- Purchase draft creation after server-side evidence validation.
- Function Calling Agent Loop in a later phase.
- Trace persistence, task state, human approval, formal order, permissions, and audit logs in later phases.
- Algorithm evaluation with honest raw results.

## Business Rules

- Forecast shortage = `max(0, forecast demand + safety stock - available supply)`.
- Available supply currently excludes in-transit supply in Phase 1 and must be documented as a simplification.
- Supplier recommendation filters by active status, material relationship, MOQ, lead time, capacity, and budget before scoring.
- Price and complaint metrics are cost metrics. Delivery and quality are benefit metrics.
- Backend calculates all numerical results and amounts.

## Deferred

- Purchase draft persistence is deferred to the next Phase 1 slice.
- Prophet integration after the baseline evaluator exists.
- Real LLM provider and Function Calling after deterministic business flow.
- Minimal frontend after stable APIs.
- Real external supplier systems, payment, email, RAG, MCP service, multi-agent, LSTM, and general ERP modules are out of MVP scope.

## Evidence Requirements

- Keep raw evaluation output.
- Report demand MAPE and shortage MAPE separately.
- For zero actual shortage, do not use epsilon to claim MAPE; report MAE/WAPE and coverage.
- Recommendation labels must not be generated by the same scoring formula under evaluation.
